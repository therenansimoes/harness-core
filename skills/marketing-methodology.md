## Marketing methodology

- Name audience → job-to-be-done → offer in one sentence each before writing copy.
- One primary CTA per surface. Secondary links do not compete with the CTA.
- Message test: would a stranger understand the value in 5 seconds without brand lore?
- Prefer concrete claims (what / for whom / outcome) over adjectives.
- Define the conversion metric up front (signup, purchase, reply) and how it is measured.
- Do not invent testimonials, prices, or legal claims — leave placeholders marked `TODO(human)`.

## Done when
- Audience, job-to-be-done, and offer named; single primary CTA; conversion metric defined; no invented claims (placeholders for human-filled data).
